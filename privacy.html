Mindloom / Privacy

Private by design.

Mindloom is an offline mental-model card app. The app does not require an account, does not send your reading activity to a server, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Last updatedSeptember 27, 2026

Short versionYour thoughts stay on your device.

What Mindloom collects

Mindloom does not collect personal information, account details, contact lists, location data, browsing history, reading history, or usage analytics.

What stays on your device

The current version is intentionally simple. The bundled mental-model library and your interactions with the cards are handled locally on your device. Mindloom does not sync this information to a cloud service.

Network access

Mindloom is designed to work without an internet connection. It does not use a remote API, artificial intelligence service, advertising SDK, or third-party tracking service.

Children’s privacy

Mindloom does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app does not require an account or collect personal data, there is no personal profile to create.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will also change.

Questions?
For privacy questions, contact support@mindloom.app.
