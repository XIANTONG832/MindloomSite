Troubleshooting

Frequently asked questions.

Does Mindloom require an internet connection?

No. The app is designed to work offline. Its mental-model content is bundled with the app.

Does Mindloom use AI?

No. Mindloom is a curated card-reading experience and does not call an AI service.

Can I export or sync my notes?

The current version intentionally keeps the experience focused on reading and reflection. It does not include export or cloud sync.

How do I report a content issue?

Email support with the model title and a short description of the issue. A screenshot is helpful when the issue is visual.

How do I request a feature?

Send the idea to support@mindloom.app with the subject “Feature request”. Explain the problem you want the feature to solve.
