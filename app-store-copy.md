## Full description

Mindloom is a pocket library for better thinking.

When a decision feels complicated, the right mental model can make the hidden structure visible. Mindloom turns useful ideas from psychology, systems thinking, science, and decision-making into a calm card ritual you can return to anywhere.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Read one model at a time, without a feed or an account.
• Flip each card to reveal its definition, context, classic example, and reflection prompt.
• Move to the next card whenever you want a fresh lens.
• Browse the full library by category or search for a specific idea.
• Explore the lattice to see how models connect across human nature, systems, science, decisions, and action.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT

Mindloom is deliberately small. It does not try to become another productivity system, social network, or endless content stream. The goal is one useful shift in attention: a better question, a clearer tradeoff, or a more accurate view of what is happening.

OFFLINE BY DEFAULT

No account. No ads. No tracking. No AI. No network connection required. Mindloom is designed to keep the reading experience private, focused, and available whenever you need it.

Start with one card. Carry one better lens into the day.
